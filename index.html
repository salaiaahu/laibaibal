        App Hmandaan

        Baibal Hmandaan

        • Mah Baibal App ah hin a um ciami leng ah na duhmi Baibal le commentary paoh .db si lo le .sqlite3 format in nangmah tein ii chiah khawh a si.
        • Na duhmi version thim piak, cun relnak (chapter) le cang (verse) thim piak.
        • Relnak (chapter) 1, 2, 3 ti in thlen na duh a si ahcun a cunglei is "<" le ">" kha hmet hna. A si lo le orhlei le kehlei ah thai law a mah tein aa thleng ko lai.
        • Baibal uk khat hnu uk khat, Genesis, Exodus ti bantuk in thlen na duh ahcun a cunglei i Baibal min Gen, Exo ti bantuk aa tial mi kha hmet law na relduh mi thimnak a lang lai. A silole na kut dong pahnih in orhlei kehlei ah thai law cauk a changchang tein an ii thleng ko lai.
        • Baibla cang (verse) kha hmet law note tialnak, bookmark tuahnak, highlight tuahnak, share tuahnak le hmanthlak sernak pawl an chuak lai.
        • Baibal version pahnih voikhat ah relti na duh ahcun a cunglei i te kha hmet.
        • Commentary (Baibal Hrilhfiah) relchih na duh ahcun a cunglei i te kha hmet.
        • Na note, bookmark le highlight pawl na save ciami zoh na duh ahcun side menu i My Data timi kha hmet law mah ka ah an um dih ko lai.
        • Na duhmi biafang (word) le biatlang (sentence) paoh kawl khawh a si. Na kawl duh mi paoh aa cunglei i kha hmet law box chung khan tial, cun "Kawl" timi kha hmet. Na duh ahcun na relio mi cauk chung telawng zongah kawl khawh a si, cun Biakam Hlun, Biakam Thar, si lo le Baibal dihlak chung tiang zong na duh ning paoh in kawl khawh dih a si.
